# Commercial Demo Storyboard

## Scene 1 — The problem
Show the same business request as a traditional "generate a page" task: many framework choices, callbacks, state and styling decisions.

## Scene 2 — Ask Eidos
Prompt: "Build an executive order-risk review. Focus humans only on exceptions, show revenue exposure, add an approval decision and a process view."

The LLM side discovers semantic capabilities and emits a compact Experience Proposal.

## Scene 3 — Deterministic gate
Show validation as a separate stage. Deliberately introduce an invalid capability or hide decision-critical evidence. Eidos rejects it without asking an LLM to guess.

## Scene 4 — Realized experience
Render a polished operations cockpit: KPIs, exception queue, evidence, BI chart, decision panel and process map.

## Scene 5 — Human direct manipulation
Drag KPI/decision panels, compact the layout, pin a region and save the view. Explain that this does not require an LLM round trip.

## Scene 6 — EC adaptation
Switch between Standard, Operations Manager, Finance Reviewer and Shared Review contexts. Structural personalization settles around stable regions; invariant evidence remains.

## Scene 7 — Collaboration
Enter Shared Review. Shared evidence, decision state and focus stay aligned while personal assistance remains peripheral.

## Scene 8 — Beyond web forms
Switch to BI / Diagram / Spatial capability examples to show Eidos is an Experience system, not a form library.

## Closing
"LLM proposes. Eidos validates. Humans decide. Host executes."
