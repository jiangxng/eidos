# Eidos Evolution Roadmap

## H1 — Deterministic LLM-native foundation
Authoritative context, capability catalog, strict contracts, structured diagnostics, runtime/host boundary, renderer registry.

## H2 — Capability-driven runtime
Promote legacy components into canonical capabilities; validate composition and compatibility.

## H3 — Adaptive experience
ExperienceContext, role/personal/shared modes, constrained page customization, stability classes, EC hints.

## H4 — Decision and exception experience
Decision panel, evidence, diff, impact preview, exception queue, uncertainty, human-in-the-loop review, attention budgets.

## H5 — Multi-terminal experience
Web, mobile/native, large screen, terminal, voice, agent-mediated UI.

## H6 — Mature personal experience
EC increasingly understands the person while Eidos remains deterministic and collaboration-safe.
