# Eidos Go-To-Market

## Product story

Eidos is not sold as another UI component library.

**Eidos is the deterministic experience system that lets LLMs assemble reliable, high-quality human interfaces from explicit capabilities instead of repeatedly generating application code.**

The sales story has three layers:

1. **For AI product teams** — reduce generated UI code, repair cycles and hidden framework knowledge.
2. **For enterprises** — keep business truth and execution in the Host while allowing AI to compose experiences safely.
3. **For humans** — get adaptive, decision-focused experiences while retaining direct control over simple customization.

## Demo promise

A credible Eidos demo must visibly prove all of these:

- an LLM can discover capabilities without source-code archaeology;
- natural-language intent can become an explicit Experience Proposal;
- Eidos validates the proposal deterministically;
- Eidos renders the same semantic contract through a controlled runtime;
- invalid proposals fail closed with actionable diagnostics;
- humans can directly drag/reorder/resize where that is faster;
- explicit human choices can override learned personalization;
- shared collaboration preserves a common reference;
- the same capability system extends beyond forms into BI, diagrams and spatial/3D.

## Audience-specific message

### CTO / AI Platform
"Give agents a small, typed, verifiable UI language instead of an unlimited code-generation surface."

### Product / Operations
"Generate task-specific decision experiences, not another fixed ERP page."

### Front-end engineering
"Keep renderer complexity, lifecycle quirks and accessibility rules inside tested Eidos cores."

### Enterprise buyer
"AI proposes; deterministic Eidos validates and realizes; your Host remains the owner of truth and execution."

## Commercial packaging direction

- **Eidos Core** — contracts, validation, runtime, capability discovery.
- **Eidos Component & Capability Packs** — Forms/Data, Decision, BI, Diagram, Spatial, Collaboration.
- **Eidos Studio** — human direct customization, preview, contract inspector, capability explorer.
- **Eidos Enterprise** — policy packs, audit, compatibility governance, private capability registry, observability.
- **Experience Compiler integration** — separate intelligence layer; not required for Eidos correctness.

## Proof metrics for future benchmark pages

- intent-to-valid-experience latency;
- generated code/token reduction vs direct framework generation;
- invalid proposal rate;
- repair-cycle count;
- capability reuse rate;
- deterministic validation coverage;
- accessibility conformance;
- renderer consistency;
- time for a human to make simple direct customizations.
